## Question 4 (Multiple Choice) - Core Problem Focus
**Think about a category you NEVER buy on Blinkit (e.g., Beauty, Electronics, or Toys). What is the primary reason you don't buy it there?**
- Trust/Quality: I don't trust the quality or authenticity of those items on a quick-commerce app.
- Awareness: I honestly didn't know Blinkit sold those items or had a good variety.
- Price: I assume they are more expensive compared to Amazon, Nykaa, etc.
- Habit: I just never think of Blinkit for those items; I use it strictly for groceries/emergencies.
- Selection: The options/brands available seem limited compared to specialized apps.
