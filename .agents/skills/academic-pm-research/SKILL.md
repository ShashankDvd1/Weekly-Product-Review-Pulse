# IDE Skill: Academic PM & Strategy Research

Use this skill when generating reports, case studies, or slide presentations (like the Executive Deck or Viva Defense scorecards).

## 1. Analysis Framework
* **Assumption Audit**: Before presenting an insight, list what assumptions are made, what evidence validates them, and what info is missing.
* **Root Cause Mapping**: Distinguish symptoms (e.g. "users avoid beauty category") from behavioral root causes (e.g. "lack of trust in fresh items", "selection bias").
* **Behavioral Dynamics**: Account for consumer habit loops, emergency triggers, and friction points in the user experience.

## 2. Executive Deck Presentation Standards
When compiling slides or slide data:
* **One Core Question**: Every slide or slide section must answer exactly one critical question.
* **Logical Flow**: Arrange slide cards so the conclusion naturally follows from the raw review evidence.
* **Argument Strength**: Do not use vague terms like "good feedback" or "bad UX". Instead, state:
  * Number of mentions and source diversity (e.g., 20+ reviews across App Store and Reddit).
  * Exact verbatim quotes as evidence.
  * Direct actionability and target metrics affected.

## 3. Writing & Tone
* Maintain a highly analytical, objective, and precise tone.
* Eliminate filler words, marketing fluff, and generic PM buzzwords.
